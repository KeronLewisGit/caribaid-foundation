Our work

Care for the child. A way back to school for the mother.

Four programmes, run by volunteers in Saint Lucia.

How a free daycare place changes things.

The same three steps, one family at a time.

  1. 1

    A young mother reaches us

    Often she is still of school age, on her own, and has had to leave school to mind her baby.

  2. 2

    Her child gets a place

    Caribaid pays for the child’s place at a daycare, with meals, at no cost to the family.

  3. 3

    She goes back to school

    With her child safe and fed during the day, she can finish her education or take up work.

Programme 01

Free daycare places

We pay for daycare places for the children of young single mothers and families who cannot afford the fees. The child is looked after during the day; the mother is free to study or work.

  • For children of single, low-income mothers
  • No cost to the family
  • The number of places depends on what we are given
Programme 02

The feeding programme

A child who has eaten can play, listen and learn. Our feeding programme provides meals for the children we support, with the help of volunteer cooks and donated groceries.

  • Meals for children at daycare
  • Cooked by volunteers
  • Dry goods and fresh produce always welcome
Programme 03

Clothes and school supplies

Good used clothes, shoes, school bags, exercise books and pencils are collected and passed on to families who need them.

  • Clean clothes and shoes for babies and children
  • Books, bags, pencils and crayons
  • Drop off in Castries or ask us to collect
Programme 04

A boot camp for young boys

Boys need steady adults too. Our mentors run a boot camp that gives young boys structure, discipline and someone to talk to.

  • Led by volunteer mentors
  • For school-age boys
  • Mentors and special-aid teachers wanted

What we are working towards

A free daycare with room for fifty children.

Today Caribaid pays for places at private daycares. Our goal is a daycare of our own, free to the families who need it most, so that no young mother has to choose between her child and her schooling.

  • Building materials: blocks, cement, steel, roofing
  • Sponsors, in Saint Lucia or abroad
  • Skilled hands: builders, electricians, plumbers
Help us build it

50 places. One block for each child the daycare will hold.
